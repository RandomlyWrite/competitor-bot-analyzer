# Degen Bot Feature Taxonomy & Scoring Rubric

Use this as reference when building the Feature Matrix or scoring gaps. Categories are tailored to provably fair Telegram gambling bots in small friend-group / tournament scenes. Scores are 1-10 where 10 = industry-leading or your aspirational standard.

## 1. Provably Fair Implementation Depth
**What good looks like**: Transparent seed system with easy user verification UX, timing of reveals documented, optional on-chain commitment or public hash logs, simulator or step-by-step proof UI. Players can actually audit a round in <30 seconds without a PhD.

**Common competitor failures**: "Provably fair" as static marketing claim in bio. Buried /help text with no UX. No client seed input. Seed rotation unclear. No way to verify past rounds easily. "Trust me bro" energy.

**Scoring examples**:
- 1-3: Checkbox claim only, no practical verification path.
- 4-6: Basic hash reveal but poor UX or incomplete (missing client seed control).
- 7-9: Solid UX + public logs or simulator.
- 10: Theatrical + educational verification ritual that makes fairness feel like part of the game (your opportunity).

**Gap signal**: Almost every bot scores low here. Your edge = make verification a dark ritual with Degen Master Blueprint visuals and sarcastic narration.

## 2. Bankroll Discipline & Risk Tools
**What good looks like**: Session history with P/L graphs, voluntary deposit/loss limits, cool-off timers, tilt detection (behavioral or self-reported), "future self" intervention messages, variance simulators, post-session reflection prompts. Tools that help degens last longer without feeling preached at.

**Common competitor failures**: Zero tools. Pure "gamble more" design. No history beyond last 10 rounds. No limits. No tilt language. Leads to quick burnout and group death.

**Scoring examples**:
- 1-2: Nothing (vast majority).
- 3-5: Basic history or manual /stats.
- 6-8: Limits + basic tracking.
- 9-10: Integrated with dark humor reflection, predictive tilt roasts, exportable to external analysis (your mclam + likes-compiler stack).

**Gap signal**: Highest leverage gap in the entire space. Degens claim they want chaos but quietly crave guardrails delivered with a middle finger and a laugh. Your productive degen angle wins here.

## 3. Games Portfolio & Mechanics
**Core games to track**: Dice (classic), Crash, Plinko, Mines, Blackjack, Roulette, Baccarat, Custom/Themed, Poker (play-money tournaments), Group/shared pot variants.

**Themed/Custom bonus**: Story-driven (Vegas heist, Degen Park characters), progression systems, ARG elements.

**Gap signal**: Saturated basic dice/crash. Underserved: high-quality small-group poker orchestration, cross-game progression, narrative campaigns that tie multiple sessions together.

## 4. Social, Group & Tournament Features
**What good looks like**: Seamless Telegram group integration, persistent player profiles across sessions, friend challenges, shared leaderboards or pots, tournament brackets with visuals, spectator modes, drama tools (confessions, roasts, tribunals).

**Common failures**: Bots that work in DMs or single chats but die in groups. No persistent memory. No bracket visuals. No way to run a 5-8 person tournament without manual coordination.

**Gap signal**: Your poker group use case is gold. Competitors rarely optimize for tight friend circles with history and inside jokes. Tournament-hype-producer + graphic-novel-architect pipeline gives you visuals no one else has.

## 5. Creative, Narrative & Dark Humor Layer
**What good looks like**: Consistent character voice or world (Degen Master Blueprint level), gonzo narration, productive roasts, visual storytelling (custom decks, animations, obituary comics, mindscape decay themes), full-bleed moody art direction. Tone that makes losing almost as entertaining as winning.

**Common failures**: Bland corporate copy or low-effort stolen memes. No character consistency. No visual identity beyond a logo. Zero narrative threading between sessions. Feels like a slot machine in a spreadsheet.

**Scoring examples**:
- 1-3: Generic or absent.
- 4-6: Some emojis or basic roast lines.
- 7-9: Distinct voice + recurring characters.
- 10: Full Degen Master Blueprint enforcement — rubber-hose, gothic surrealism, Fear and Loathing energy, integrated with your illustration skills.

**Gap signal**: Your biggest moat. Most competitors have the creative depth of a gas station hot dog. You already have the pipeline (degen-illustrated-comics, degen-park-portrait, graphic-novel-architect). Use it.

## 6. Analytics, Data Export & Forensics
**What good looks like**: Rich play history export (JSON, CSV, direct Notion sync), tilt/leak analysis, pattern mining (M-CLAM style six-vector), community architecture insights, likes/preferences compiler from chat behavior, external dashboard integration.

**Common failures**: Siloed data. No export. No self-analysis tools. Players have no idea why they tilt or what their real winrate is over time.

**Gap signal**: Extremely rare. Your mclam-analyzer + telegram-likes-compiler + Notion poker dashboard is years ahead. A bot that feeds data into your forensic stack (or lets the group run collective M-CLAM on their own play) is a killer feature.

## 7. Harm Reduction / Self-Improvement Integration
**What good looks like**: Any mechanics or language that acknowledges degen psychology and offers productive outlets: reflection logs, "Anatomy of Digital Stromboli" style auto-reports, tilt intervention with humor, discipline badges or progression for smart bankroll moves, optional charity rake or "bust fund" mechanics, future-self letters that actually land because they're funny and specific.

**Common failures**: Either zero (pure casino) or preachy TED-talk tone that degens immediately mute. No integration with actual play data.

**Gap signal**: Near-total absence. This is the "productive" half of your brand that competitors can't fake because they lack the dark humor delivery system and the data tools to make it personal.

## 8. Technical Extensibility & Retention
**What good looks like**: Mini App support, clean webhooks, API for third-party tools, multi-bot swarm coordination, persistent memory across chats, easy onboarding for new group members, cross-bot events or shared progression.

**Gap signal**: Most are single-purpose and brittle. Your swarm / guardian / inter-bot ideas from past sessions are still wide open territory.

## Quick Rubric for Prioritizing Gaps
When scoring a gap for the user:
- **User Alignment (1-10)**: How well does it fit existing poker group, Degen Master Blueprint visuals, mclam/likes-compiler data, graphic novel interests?
- **Differentiation (1-10)**: How many competitors are even attempting this? (Low score = crowded, high = wide open)
- **Feasibility (1-10)**: Can it be prototyped quickly as Mini App or bot command using current stack?
- **Retention Impact (1-10)**: Will this make players open the bot more often, stay in group longer, or invite friends?
- Composite = average or weighted. Top 3-5 composites become the Opportunity Concepts.

## Anti-Patterns to Call Out in Critiques
- "Provably fair" with no UX = future rug accusation waiting to happen.
- No bankroll tools = group will eventually implode from tilt spirals and one guy going broke every session.
- Bland creative = zero word-of-mouth or emotional attachment. Players treat it as disposable.
- Data silo = players can't do the deep self-reflection that actually improves play (and keeps them coming back).
- Preachy harm reduction without humor = instant ignore.
- Token launch or "ecosystem" promises = red flag unless extremely well executed (rare).

Use this taxonomy to keep analysis consistent and ruthless. Update it when new patterns emerge from real competitor scans.
